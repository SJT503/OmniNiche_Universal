## 3. Pass 2 Deep Dissection — Narrative Craftsmanship, Logical Progression & Editor/Reviewer Ergonomics

### 3.1 Triple-Lens & Six-Question Narrative Teardown (`journal-benchmark-review` Step 3 & Step 4)

1. **Lens A — Macro-Architecture & Pacing**:
   - *Nature Medicine* flagship computational-translational articles (`E2 COMPASS`, `E4 TxGNN`) devote **`~900–1,150` words (`4–5` paragraphs)** to the Introduction so clinical readers understand *why existing biomarkers/atlases fail biologically* before encountering any computational formalism, and divide Results into **`6–7` single-thesis subsections (`~450–750` words each)** rather than 3 dense 900-word blocks.
2. **Lens B — Paragraph Internal Logic ("Phenomenon -> Paradox -> Test -> Quantitative Proof -> Biological Meaning")**:
   - Every Results subsection in `E1–E3` opens with a **15–24 word plain-language clinical/biological motivation sentence** (e.g., `E2`: *"To be clinically useful, predictive models must generalize beyond individual studies across cancer types, treatment regimens and immune checkpoint targets despite biological and technical heterogeneity."*), states the exact experimental/validation design in sentence 2, delivers the headline effect size + CI + *P*-value in sentences 3–4, and closes with a concrete disease/drug or patient-subgroup anchor (`LUAD`, `IMvigor210` `TGFβ`-high inflamed non-responders, `SERPINC1`/`TREM2` reversal).
3. **Lens C — Sentence Ergonomics & Accessibility ("既专业易懂，又符合编辑和审稿人的阅读习惯")**:
   - Mean sentence length in `E1–E3` Results is **`19–24` words** (`95%` of sentences `< 38` words). Complex statistical controls (e.g., `E1` Cochran–Mantel–Haenszel / Simpson's paradox; `E2` LOCO vs LOPO vs 240 cohort transfers; `E3` natural-effects mediation controlling for 5 baseline confounders) are introduced by **what confounder they rule out in plain English**, rather than burying the reader in nested mathematical clauses.
4. **Six Narrative Craft Answers From `E1–E3`**:
   - **Q1 (Opening Hook)**: Anchor on clinical attrition cost (`~90%` failure) + concrete paradoxes where current biomarkers fail (`high-TMB/inflamed non-responders` in `E2`; genetically supported or high-$\tau_0$ Phase 3 failures in `E1`/OmniNiche).
   - **Q2 (Gap Framing)**: Frame prior work respectfully as *necessary but incomplete* (`E2`: TMB/PD-L1/TIDE capture single axes but lack generalizable tumor–stroma–immune state representation; OmniNiche: genetics and healthy $\tau_0$ establish causal target validity in healthy tissue, but cannot see disease-induced lineage drift or macromolecular ECM steric exclusion).
   - **Q3 (Handling Counter-Examples / Imperfections)**: Report counter-examples proactively and non-defensively (`E2` candidly notes baselines beat COMPASS in some small intra-cohort PR settings; `E3` notes Phase 2 histological fibrosis stage regression missed significance at 72 weeks before showing SomaSignal/ESSENCE reversal; OmniNiche candidly explains `F3 simtuzumab` & `elafibranor` in `Fig. 4c` and homogenized `NSCLC bulk` rank AUROC vs best Brier `0.2250` in `Fig. 5d`, plus `PBMC sepsis` intravascular boundary condition `AUROC = 0.4793`).
   - **Q4 (Literature Reconciliation in Discussion)**: Connect computational predictions directly to independent wet-lab pathology (`E3 Jara et al. 2025` RNAscope/IHC demonstration of **zero `GLP1R` expression in human hepatocytes, stellate cells, and liver macrophages** alongside systemic `TREM2`/ECM signature reversal directly validates OmniNiche's mechanistic classification of `semaglutide` in `ESSENCE`!).
   - **Q5 (Limitations & Bias Direction)**: State 3–4 concrete limitations with explicit bias direction, empirical sensitivity bounds already tested, and clinical deployment guardrails (`E2`: *"Predictions by COMPASS or similar models should never be used alone to deny immunotherapy"*).
   - **Q6 (Closing Cadence)**: End without `"In conclusion"`/`"In summary"` clichés, landing on a calm, forward-looking translational principle.
