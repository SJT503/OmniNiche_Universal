## 2. Four-Perspective Teardown of *Nature Medicine* & *Science* Acceptance Standards

### 2.1 *Nature Medicine* Handling Editor Lens (Why Papers Survive Triage vs Desk-Reject)
1. **Immediate Clinical Problem Framing, Zero Internal Partitioning Jargon**:
   - Every exemplar opens with an unmistakable, self-contained clinical bottleneck in Sentence 1–2:
     - `E2` (*TxGNN*): *"Drug repurposing identifies new therapeutic uses for approved drugs, yet most diseases lack effective treatments..."*
     - `E6` (*SCORPIO*): *"Immune checkpoint inhibitors produce durable responses in a subset of patients with cancer, but predicting clinical benefit before treatment remains challenging..."*
     - `E7` (*COMPASS*): *"Immune checkpoint inhibitors (ICIs) are a standard treatment across cancers, yet most patients do not respond, and existing biomarkers generalize poorly across tumor types and therapies."*
   - **Zero negative/engineering scope labels**: None of `E1–E9` define their cohort by what was excluded for another paper (never internal partitioning labels). When `E6` or `E9` focus on solid tumors or systemic organs, they state the positive biomedical scope directly (*"12 systemic and parenchymal organ disease axes"*) and note specialized neurovascular exclusions cleanly in *Methods*.
2. **A Named, Memorable Quantitative Index / Framework**:
   - *Nature Medicine* papers anchor clinical translation around a crisp, interpretable handle (`TxGNN`, `SCORPIO`, `COMPASS`, `HEX/MICA`), never leaving the core equation nameless (`"Drift x Barrier"`). Introducing **Dynamic Niche Accessibility (`DNAcc`)** inside **OmniNiche** aligns directly with this editorial convention.
3. **Actionable Clinical Decision Utility Beyond Raw AUROC**:
   - Editors ask: *"If a trial sponsor, hepatologist, or medical oncologist uses `DNAcc` / OmniNiche tomorrow, how many futile Phase III trials or ineffective monotherapies are avoided?"* `E7` (*COMPASS*) pairs discrimination with **Brier probability calibration** and **Decision Curve Analysis (DCA) net benefit across threshold probabilities**, while `E1` (*Minikel*) and `E6` (*SCORPIO*) quantify concrete **tier/quartile clinical progression enrichment (`Q4/Q1`)**.

### 2.2 Biostatistics & Machine-Learning Reviewer Lens (Confounding, Leakage, Uncertainty & Missingness)
1. **Shortcut-Learning & Target-Leakage Prevention (`E2` *TxGNN* & `E7` *COMPASS*)**:
   - `E2` explicitly demonstrates that standard random cross-validation inflates AUROC because shared targets/drugs appear in both training and test folds ("shortcut learning"), and therefore pairs standard CV with **strict unseen-group / zero-shot splits** (`GroupKFold` by `primary_target_gene` in Pillar I; bidirectional `3×3` Leave-One-Organ-Out in Pillar II; strictly pre-treatment zero-refit patient scoring in Pillar III).
2. **Orthogonal Stepwise Component Ablation (`E2` & `E8`)**:
   - Every multi-term predictor in `E2`, `E7`, and `E8` undergoes a clean monotonic ablation isolating each biological module.
   - *Critical v1 Audit Discovery*: In Stage II v1 (`omniniche/stage2/pan_disease_drift_law.py` lines 218–222), `p_drift_only` only fed `[tau0, tau_early, delta_tau]` (`AUROC = 0.6073` vs `0.6081` for `tau_early`), and then `X_omni` jumped straight to `0.6728` by bundling `vital_bystander_hazard`, `barrier_compatibility`, and `tau_early * barrier` in one leap while omitting `compartment_switch` and `dynamic_retention_score` from the drift step! In v2.0, we must implement a transparent **6-step orthogonal ablation** (`M1: Healthy tau0` → `M2: +Vital-Organ Bystander` → `M3: +Disease Early tau` → `M4: +Dynamic Drift [Delta_tau + Dynamic Retention]` → `M5: +Lineage Compartment Switch` → `M6: +Biophysical Modality Barrier = Full DNAcc`) under both 5-fold Stratified CV and unseen-target `GroupKFold`.
3. **Uncertainty Intervals & Sample-Size-Appropriate Hypothesis Tests (`E1`, `E7`, `E8`)**:
   - `E8` (*HEX/MICA*) reports **non-parametric bootstrap 95% CIs (`1,000–2,000` resamples)** on every AUROC, AUPRC, and C-index bar/forest point, and tests paired model gains rigorously.
   - For large clinical-trial cohorts (`n = 11,843`, `5,815`, `23,967`), paired AUROC gains require **exact paired DeLong tests** (`DeLong et al., Biometrics 1988`).
   - For small translational cohorts (`n = 14` Phase IIb/III antifibrotic trials; `n = 8` BCC; `n = 14` NSCLC scRNA; `n = 24` NSCLC pre-treatment bulk; `n = 32` pooled pre-treatment biopsies), asymptotic normal approximations are fragile; **exact two-sided 10,000-label-permutation *P*-values** paired with **2,000-iteration stratified bootstrap 95% CIs** provide airtight small-sample inference.
4. **Multivariable Confounder Control & Polypharmacology Sensitivity (`E1`, `E6`, `E8`)**:
   - `E1` (*Minikel*) tests whether genetic effect sizes survive **therapy-area stratification**, **temporal discovery-era control**, and **single-target-only (`n_targets == 1`) restriction** (ruling out polypharmacology artifacts).
   - `E6` (*SCORPIO*) and `E8` (*HEX/MICA*) run multivariable regressions proving their score remains an independent predictor (`P < 1e-10`) *after* adjusting for clinical covariates. Because `DNAcc` incorporates modality-barrier compatibility across 12 axes, a sharp reviewer will demand a **multivariable fixed-effects logistic regression adjusting simultaneously for `disease_axis`, `clinical_phase`, `drug_modality`, and `start_year` calendar era**, plus a **single-target (`n_targets == 1`) vs multi-target (`n_targets >= 2`) subgroup audit**.
5. **Classifier Validation & Missing-Outcome (`3,118` Unreported Stops) Sensitivity (`E1` & `E6`)**:
   - Because `41.0%` (`2,706/6,595`) administrative vs `11.7%` (`771/6,595`) explicit scientific stoppage is a headline finding while `47.3%` (`3,118/6,595`) of stopped trials lack granular `why_stopped` text on ClinicalTrials.gov, reviewers will demand:
     1. A **stratified human-curated benchmark audit (`n >= 200` stopped trials)** reporting per-class precision, recall, macro-F1, `4×4` confusion matrix, and Cohen's $\kappa$;
     2. A **4-regime missingness sensitivity analysis** across the `3,118` unreported stops (`Regime 1`: primary deconfounded explicit outcomes `n = 11,843`; `Regime 2`: ultra-conservative worst-case treating all `3,118` unreported stops as scientific failures `n = 14,961`; `Regime 3`: inverse-probability-weighted [`IPW`] propensity re-estimation across phase/modality/sponsor/era; `Regime 4`: `HasResults == True` posted-results-only subset).

### 2.3 Translational Pharmacology & Domain Expert Lens (Hepatology, Pulmonology, Nephrology & Immuno-Oncology)
1. **Accurate Pharmacology of `Semaglutide` (`GLP1R`, `ESSENCE`) vs `Resmetirom` (`THRB`, `MAESTRO-NASH`) (`E4` *Nat. Med.* 2025)**:
   - *Critical v1 Audit Discovery*: In `omniniche/stage2/fibrosis_mash_deconvolution.py` line 53, `Semaglutide` was tagged `"modality": "small_molecule"`, and v1 Discussion line 61 grouped `THRB` and `GLP1R` together as *"targets that retain or gain parenchymal and stellate specificity"*. `E4` (Jara et al., *Nat. Med.* 2025, `PMID: 40691365`) proved by RNAscope and IHC across 26 human liver biopsies that **`GLP1R` is undetectable on human hepatocytes, stellate cells, and Kupffer cells**, and that semaglutide (`4.11 kDa` acylated peptide, hydrodynamic radius `~1.5 nm`, non-IgG low-MW peptide that bypasses `150 kDa` IgG steric trapping) resolves MASH and suppresses circulating fibrotic mediators (`ADAMTSL2`, `TREM2`, `CD163`) via **systemic cardiometabolic and extrahepatic-to-parenchymal inflammatory unloading**! Fixing `Semaglutide`'s modality label to `"peptide"` (`< 5 kDa` low-MW non-IgG class) and contrasting intrahepatic `THRB` retention (`Resmetirom`) against extrahepatic metabolic unloading (`Semaglutide`) citing `PMID: 40691365` eliminates a major hepatology vulnerability.
2. **Bidirectional `3×3` Cross-Organ Fibrosis Transfer**:
   - In v1, transfer was only tested unidirectionally (`Liver -> Lung & Kidney`). Reviewers will ask: *"Is the 3-compartment macrophage–myofibroblast–parenchyma grammar genuinely pan-organ symmetric, or does calibration only work from liver?"* Computing the full **`3×3` directional + Leave-One-Organ-Out (`Liver -> Lung/Kidney`, `Lung -> Liver/Kidney`, `Kidney -> Liver/Lung`) transfer matrix** across all 64 primary human biopsies and 14 trials proves true organ-invariant dynamical conservation.
3. **12-Section `21,796`-Spot Visium Continuous Radial Distance Decay (`0–500 µm`) & Dual Contrasting Tissue Archetypes (`E8` & `E9`)**:
   - `E8` (*HEX/MICA*) pairs cohort-wide spatial statistics with **side-by-side contrasting high-risk (`FAP+/collagen+` excluded) vs low-risk (`CD8+/GZMB+` infiltrated) patient tissue maps** (`Fig. 6f`), and `E9` (*Klughammer/Regev*) plots **continuous `0–500 µm` spatial distance decay curves** (`Fig. 4c`). Upgrading our `Fig. 5a,b` from a single breast tumor section + 2-bar mean comparison to **dual contrasting excluded vs infiltrated Visium sections** + **continuous `0` to `5+` spot-pitch (`0–500 µm`) radial decay curves across all 12 human sections** directly meets *Nature Medicine* / *Science* spatial standards.
4. **Exact Code-to-Methods Mathematical Parity**:
   - *Critical v1 Audit Discovery*:
     - `LangevinNicheSolver._drift_batch` (`langevin_solver.py` lines 34–35, 97–98) integrates the **state-bounded carrying-capacity SDE** with $(1 - S_j)$ saturation over dimensionless horizon $T = 96.0\text{ h}$ at step $\Delta t = 0.5\text{ h}$, whereas v1 *Methods* line 90–91 omitted $(1 - S_j)$ and wrote $\Delta t = 0.05\text{ h}, T = 48\text{ h}$.
