## 2. Five Independent Reviews

### Role 1: Editor-in-Chief (EIC — *Nature Medicine*)

#### Overall Assessment
This manuscript addresses one of the most consequential unsolved problems in translational medicine: why ~85%–90% of investigational drugs fail in Phase II–III clinical trials even when supported by germline human genetics and healthy single-cell expression specificity ($\tau_0$). Across 41,396 Phase II–IV clinical trials spanning 12 systemic and parenchymal organ disease axes and 237 primary human clinical specimens (>530,000 single cells, 99 strictly pre-treatment bulk biopsies, and 21,796 10x Visium spots), the authors demonstrate two orthogonal tissue-realization laws: (1) 17.8%–37.1% of drug targets switch their dominant expressing lineage during human disease progression, and (2) ~150-kDa IgG monoclonal antibodies suffer a 1.57-fold excess in explicit efficacy futility ($P = 2.26 \times 10^{-4}$) due to interstitial extracellular matrix (ECM) steric exclusion.

Compared with Stage II v2.2, the v2.3 package achieves a decisive leap in visual authority and clinical anchoring:
1. **`Fig. 1` is now a true *Nature Medicine* pure-vector architectural schematic**, integrating explicit cohort scale cards and the mathematical operator chain ($\tau_0 \to \Delta\tau \to B_{\mathrm{acc}} \to \mathrm{DNAcc}$ and the closure-free stochastic Itô-Langevin saddle-escape engine).
2. **`Fig. 4h` replaces a sparse binary table with a continuous $13 \times 13$ two-dimensional dose-response Bliss synergy surface and 50% remission isobologram**, crystallizing the orthogonal rescue rule in locked F4 cirrhosis.
3. **`Fig. 5h` adds real overall survival follow-up across $n = 74$ strictly pre-treatment anti-PD-1 monotherapy patients (`GSE78220` + `GSE91061`, 43 deaths)**, showing significant mortality stratification by pre-treatment stromal barrier burden ($\mathrm{HR} = 1.95$, log-rank $P = 0.0279$) where `PD-L1` ($P = 0.95$) and `18-gene GEP` ($P = 0.73$) fail.

The study is conceptually original, broad in human clinical scale, and unusually disciplined in declaring its anatomical and platform boundaries (peripheral interstitial ECM vs blood-brain barrier transport; intravascular PBMC sepsis null boundary; bulk lysis attenuation in melanoma; and responsible clinical AI guardrails). However, before formal acceptance, the authors must tighten **one important claim-attribution ambiguity in the Abstract and Table 2 regarding the $n = 74$ survival predictor** and resolve **two localized code-to-figure-legend mismatches** identified by our technical reviewers. None of these require new wet-lab experiments or new external datasets; all can be closed deterministically from the existing codebase.
