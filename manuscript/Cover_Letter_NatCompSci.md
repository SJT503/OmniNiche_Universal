**Re: Modeling dynamic tissue accessibility predicts clinical trial outcomes across human organs**

Please find enclosed our Article manuscript referenced above for consideration at Nature Computational Science. Computational target prioritization today relies overwhelmingly on static equilibrium features: germline genetic associations and baseline single-cell reference atlases compiled from healthy donors. Yet more than 85% of investigational drugs entering clinical trials still fail before approval, and clinical registry outcomes themselves are heavily confounded by non-biological commercial and operational halts. Our study introduces a multi-scale computational and biophysical modeling framework that resolves how dynamic cellular lineage drift and macromolecular interstitial barrier exclusion govern late-stage clinical efficacy across human organs.

To eliminate circular registry heuristics and clinical-label overfitting, OmniNiche couples a deterministic clause-aware natural-language stoppage grammar across 41,396 interventional Phase II-IV clinical trials spanning 12 systemic and parenchymal organ disease axes (validated on 240 clinically adjudicated trials at 95.8% accuracy, Cohen's kappa = 0.941) with primary human single-cell, strictly pre-treatment bulk, 10x Visium spatial, and experimental perturbation transcriptomics across 20 cohorts and 17 NCBI GEO accessions (>530,000 single cells, 20,880 quality-filtered spatial spots, 99 pre-treatment ICB bulk biopsies, 73 human fibroblast perturbation RNA-seq samples, and 264 non-ICB clinical survival controls). Removing administrative and commercial closures (51.4% of all trial stoppages) exposes a 1.57-fold excess in explicit biological efficacy futility for ~150-kDa monoclonal antibodies relative to small molecules (P = 2.26 x 10^-4) and reveals that 17.8% to 37.1% of clinical targets switch dominant cellular lineage during human disease progression.

Three methodological and empirical advances make this work directly suited to the computational biology, biophysical modeling, and translational systems readership of Nature Computational Science. First, Dynamic Niche Accessibility (DNAcc) generalizes zero-shot across unseen target genes (Group5Fold AUROC = 0.6541) and held-out organ disease axes (Leave-One-Disease-Axis-Out AUROC = 0.6306, P = 5.19 x 10^-16), combining additively with Open Targets human genetics (AUROC = 0.7023 scientific; 0.7210 Phase II-to-III/IV maturation, P = 1.58 x 10^-10), whereas static healthy specificity adds zero predictive value once operational halts are removed. Second, compiling a closure-free, state-bounded stochastic Ito-Langevin Waddington dynamical solver exclusively from human single-cell biopsies with zero trial-outcome supervision predicts 24 SHA-256-locked Phase IIb/III antifibrotic trials across liver, lung, and kidney (AUROC = 0.8714, exact P = 0.0010; invariant to ChEMBL v33 sub-nanomolar target potency pXC50, with Leave-One-Organ-Out Platt recalibration Brier error = 0.1735 and macro-to-meso cross-scale consilience Spearman rho = 0.7861, P = 5.29 x 10^-6), while its locked 21-gene myofibroblast barrier program is directly reversed in human wet-lab RNA-seq perturbations by pirfenidone (P = 4.47 x 10^-6), selective TGFBR1/ALK5 kinase inhibitor RepSox (P = 1.10 x 10^-3), and nintedanib (P = 0.0182). Third, embedding 12-section human carcinoma 10x Visium peritumoral myCAF-TAM barrier geometry (24,000 toroidal cyclic shifts Stouffer Z = +7.82, P = 2.69 x 10^-15) stratifies 107 strictly pre-treatment anti-PD-1 biopsies (AUROC = 0.6367, P = 0.0187; single-cell TME AUROC = 0.8750) and selectively predicts mortality under anti-PD-1 monotherapy (n = 74, multivariable HR = 3.02, P = 0.0082, VanderWeele E-value = 3.68) but not across 264 non-ICB melanoma controls (multivariable HR = 0.84, P = 0.389; two-regime interaction HR = 3.60, P = 0.0059).

In keeping with rigorous computational verification and validation standards (MINSEQE and ASME V&V 40), we also report explicit physical falsification boundaries: intravascular PBMC sepsis shows expected null interstitial barrier discrimination (AUROC = 0.3822), bulk tissue homogenization compresses spatial-Langevin trajectories toward linear-combination parity on lysed biopsies, and deterministic multi-target Waddington rescue shifts are framed strictly as mechanistic, hypothesis-generating priors for biomarker-stratified combination studies.
