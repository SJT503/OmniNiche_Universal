## 2. Supplementary Notes 1–3

### Supplementary Note 1 | Unified Clause-Aware Registry Stoppage Deconvolution Grammar and 240-Trial Clinical Adjudication Protocol

Public clinical trial registries such as ClinicalTrials.gov assign identical status codes (`TERMINATED`, `WITHDRAWN`, or `SUSPENDED`) to studies halted for sponsor portfolio reprioritization, corporate restructuring, pandemic disruption, or insufficient accrual and to studies halted for biological efficacy futility or dose-limiting organ toxicity. Multi-clause free-text `whyStopped` narratives frequently combine an operational root cause with an explicit safety or efficacy negation disclaimer (for example, *"Study terminated early due to strategic portfolio reprioritization; termination was not related to any safety or efficacy concerns"*). Naive substring matchers trigger false-positive biological failure labels whenever negated safety or efficacy tokens appear anywhere in the record.

To eliminate circular heuristics and label leakage across all 41,396 unique Phase II–IV clinical trials spanning 12 systemic and parenchymal organ disease axes (9,406 stopped trials and 31,990 completed trials), OmniNiche applies a deterministic two-stage clause-aware parser (`omniniche/stage2/clinical_trials_macro_atlas.py`). First, verbatim `whyStopped` strings are segmented across punctuation (`;`, `.`) and contrastive conjunction boundaries (`but`, `however`, `although`, `whereas`, `while`, `unrelated to`, `independent of`, `with no`, `without any`, and `not due to`). Second, clauses matching explicit negation frames (`no safety concerns`, `not due to safety or efficacy`, `absence of toxicity`, `no unexpected adverse events`, or `not efficacy-related`) are stripped of biological trigger tokens prior to hierarchical outcome assignment into `Scientific_Efficacy_Futility` ($n = 476$), `Scientific_Safety_Toxicity` ($n = 317$), `Administrative_Commercial_Enrollment` ($n = 4,838$, representing 51.4% of all trial stoppages), or `Other_Unreported_Stop` ($n = 3,775$).

On the independent 240-trial clinical adjudication reference (`Manual_Clinical_Adjudication_240`: 64 efficacy futility, 54 safety/toxicity, 96 administrative/operational, and 26 unreported stops; `Supplementary Table 2`), the unified clause-aware parser achieved 95.83% overall accuracy (95% Wilson CI 92.50%–97.72%) and Cohen's $\kappa = 0.9413$ (per-class F1 = 0.9600 for efficacy futility, 0.9815 for safety/toxicity, 0.9634 for administrative halts, and 0.8929 for unreported stops), compared with 70.00% accuracy (95% CI 63.92%–75.45%), $\kappa = 0.6105$, and 43.75% administrative recall for the Naive Keyword Baseline.

### Supplementary Note 2 | Closure-Free Pseudobulk Coupling, Profile-Likelihood Structural Identifiability, and Stochastic Itô–Langevin Waddington Formulation

In single-cell and single-nucleus biopsy atlases, estimating intercellular coupling from cell-type relative proportions ($\sum_{c=1}^{K} p_c = 1$) introduces spurious negative correlations via unit-sum compositional closure when inflammatory immune infiltration dilutes parenchymal or stromal fractions. Across all 64 primary human biopsy donors spanning hepatic MASH and cirrhosis (`GSE136103` and `GSE212837`, $n = 17$ liver donors plus $4$ blood monocyte baselines), pulmonary IPF (`GSE135893`, $n = 30$), and renal CKD (`GSE271188`, $n = 17$), OmniNiche quantifies compartment states (`Parenchyma_Injury`, `SAMac_SPP1_TREM2`, and `Myofibroblast`) directly from cell-level $\log_2(\mathrm{CPM}+1)$ transcriptional module activity averaged within each donor's annotated lineage before platform z-standardization (`Supplementary Table 4`). Because transcriptional program intensity within resident myofibroblasts does not mechanically sum to unity with hepatocyte or epithelial injury intensity, compositional closure bias is eliminated (`All 64` parenchymal-myofibroblast Pearson $r = 0.45$, two-sided $P = 2.07 \times 10^{-4}$; human liver $r = 0.86$, $P = 1.20 \times 10^{-5}$).

Structural identifiability of the four directed intercellular Hill coupling weights compiled exclusively from the 17 human liver donors was audited via 25-point bilateral profile-likelihood scans ($v_{ij} \in [0.50, 1.50] \times v_{ij,\mathrm{MLE}}$) while re-optimizing the nuisance global rate scale $k_{\mathrm{scale}} \in [0.82, 1.22]$ at every grid point. All four directed edges exceeded the 95% 1-d.f. $\chi^2$ threshold ($\Delta\chi^2 > 3.841$; `Extended Data Fig. 3a` and `Supplementary Table 4`), confirming bounded parameter identifiability without clinical trial outcome supervision; excluding the three extrahepatic or hemodynamic-routed regimens (`semaglutide`, `tirzepatide`, and `dapagliflozin`) likewise preserved AUROC = 0.8673 across all 21 strictly intra-organ direct-receptor trials (versus 0.3367 for static $\tau_0$; Core-13 AUROC = 0.9500). Across 25 anatomical progression states from early F1/F2 steatohepatitis to F4 cirrhosis, cooperative positive feedback ($n = 3.6$) between $SPP1^+TREM2^+$ scar-associated macrophages and $POSTN^+$ myofibroblasts generates a saddle-node bifurcation ($s_{\mathrm{prog}} \approx 0.42\text{–}0.54$, monotherapy window cliff $t_{50} = 8.3\text{ h}$) that traps single-compartment monotherapies at 0.0% simulated remission in locked F4 cirrhosis (`s_0 = [0.854, 0.880, 0.901]`), whereas orthogonal dual perturbation pairing upstream parenchymal unloading (`THRB` or `GLP1R`) with direct small-molecule `TGFBR1/PDGFRB` or `TREM2/SPP1` modulation collapses the hysteresis saddle (99.1%–100.0% simulated remission, Bliss excess $+0.99$ to $+1.00$).

### Supplementary Note 3 | Cross-Scale Orthogonal Stress Tests (`LODO`, ChEMBL v33 Potency and `LOOO` Recalibration, 2D Visium Toroidal Shift Nulls, Pre-ICB `LOCO`, Multivariable Cox Survival, Wet-Lab `TGFBR1`/Antifibrotic Perturbation, and Non-ICB Specificity)

Five deterministic cross-scale stress tests (`omniniche/stage2/r3_orthogonal_validations.py`, `Extended Data Fig. 5a–e`, `Extended Data Fig. 6a–d`, and `Supplementary Table 6`) audited out-of-organ generalization, biochemical drug affinity, spatial tissue autocorrelation, clinical survival confounding, wet-lab pharmacological reversal, and immunotherapy specificity:

1. **Core-6 L1/L2 Shrinkage and Leave-One-Disease-Axis-Out (`LODO`) Zero-Shot Transfer (`Table_S6a`)**: Across the six directly single-cell-resolved disease axes ($n = 5,662$ scientific trials, 386 explicit biological failures across $G = 293$ targets), nested target-grouped L1 (`AUROC = 0.6325`) and L2 (`AUROC = 0.6363`) regularization stabilized finite-sample parameter variance. Holding out an entire organ disease axis during training and predicting trials in that unseen organ system via intercept-free standardized logits ($C = 0.25$) achieved pooled out-of-fold `LODO` AUROC = 0.6306 (95% CI 0.6033–0.6589) versus 0.4875 for static healthy $\tau_0$ (`+0.1431`, two-sided paired DeLong $P = 5.19 \times 10^{-16}$), generalizing across autoimmune IBD (`0.6811`), pulmonary IPF (`0.6077`, $P = 0.0335$), and solid oncology (`0.5979`, $P = 8.97 \times 10^{-9}$).
2. **Curated ChEMBL v33 Human Target Potency and Leave-One-Organ-Out (`LOOO`) Platt Recalibration (`Table_S6b`)**: Annotating all 24 Phase IIb/III antifibrotic trials with curated ChEMBL v33 human target biochemical potency ($\mathrm{pXC}_{50}$ spanning 3.52 for pirfenidone to 10.00 for lebrikizumab; mean 7.74) and modulating reference leverage via $\eta_{\mathrm{eff}}(\alpha) = \eta_0 \cdot \mathrm{clip}(1 + \alpha(\mathrm{pXC}_{50} - 7.5), 0.35, 1.55)$ preserved rank discrimination (`AUROC = 0.8714`, exact $P = 0.0019$ at $\alpha = 0.08$; `0.8643`, $P = 0.0021$ at $\alpha = 0.15$; `0.8571`, $P = 0.0027$ at $\alpha = 0.25$). High-affinity failures (`lebrikizumab` $\mathrm{pK}_d = 10.00$, `tropifexor` $\mathrm{pEC}_{50} = 9.70$, `selonsertib` $\mathrm{pIC}_{50} = 8.30$, `cenicriviroc` $\mathrm{pIC}_{50} = 8.15$) remained trapped below 0.06 remission probability due to macromolecular steric exclusion or intrahepatic lineage loss. Fitting a 1D logistic Platt calibrator strictly on the two non-target organs (`LOOO`) reduced Brier error from 0.2072 to 0.1735 (`AUROC = 0.8429`, $P = 0.0040$) and lifted fixed-0.50 balanced accuracy from 71.4% to 76.4% (81.4% when paired with ChEMBL $\alpha = 0.15$ potency).
3. **10x Visium Library-Depth and Lineage-Admixture Residualization and 24,000 Toroidal Cyclic Shift Realizations (`Table_S6c`)**: Across all 12 primary human carcinoma 10x Visium sections ($20,880$ quality-filtered spots), ordinary-least-squares residualization of spot module scores against $\log_2(\mathrm{total\ UMI}+1)$ and non-lymphoid lineage Shannon admixture entropy retained 48.3% of mean peritumoral Stromal Exclusion Index (`0.0460` of `0.0952`). To preserve exact two-dimensional spatial autocorrelation variograms within both stromal and lymphocyte fields, `CD8_Effector` coordinates were cyclically shifted across the 2D array grid (`array_row`, `array_col` modulo tissue bounding dimensions, minimum shift $\ge 3$ spot pitches) across 2,000 toroidal permutations per section ($24,000$ total spatial realizations), yielding spot-weighted Stouffer meta-analytic $Z = +7.82$ ($P = 2.69 \times 10^{-15}$).
4. **Four-Cohort Pre-ICB `LOCO` Transfer and Multivariable Breslow Cox Survival with VanderWeele E-Values (`Table_S6d`)**: Under four-cohort Leave-One-Cohort-Out (`LOCO`) evaluation across all $n = 107$ strictly pre-treatment anti-PD-1 biopsies, zero-label OmniNiche generalized across BCC (`0.8750`), NSCLC (`0.7704`), pembrolizumab melanoma (`0.5982`), and nivolumab melanoma (`0.5385`; pooled platform-aligned `LOCO` 0.5979 versus static `PD-L1` 0.5610 and `18-gene GEP` 0.5948). Across all $n = 74$ strictly pre-treatment anti-PD-1 monotherapy melanoma patients with overall survival follow-up (43 deaths), parsimonious two-covariate cohort-adjusted Breslow Cox regression (`Cox_Model1`, `EPV = 21.5`) confirmed stromal barrier hazard (`HR = 1.97`, 95% CI 1.07–3.61, $P = 0.0290$, E-value = 2.57), while full five-covariate adjustment (`Cox_Model2`, `EPV = 8.6`) controlling simultaneously for clinical cohort (`HR = 1.70`, $P = 0.238$), baseline tumor burden (`HR = 1.60` per s.d., $P = 0.0498$), static `PD-L1` (`HR = 1.19`, $P = 0.630$), and `18-gene GEP` (`HR = 1.37`, $P = 0.468$) unmasked negative confounding in inflamed-yet-excluded tumors and strengthened locked stromal barrier hazard to multivariable HR = 3.02 (95% CI 1.33–6.84, $P = 0.0082$, Harrell's $C = 0.6361$, VanderWeele E-value = 3.68, lower 95% CI E-value = 1.74), whereas continuous linear `CAF` score was non-significant (`HR = 1.08`, $P = 0.651$).
5. **Human Wet-Lab Fibroblast and Selective `TGFBR1`/ALK5i Perturbation Triad, Non-ICB Melanoma Survival Specificity Control, and 24-Trial Consilience (`Table_S6e`)**: Without refitting any signature gene or weight, the locked 21-gene OmniNiche myofibroblast barrier program was evaluated across $n = 73$ primary human fibroblast perturbation RNA-seq profiles spanning pirfenidone in activated human IPF and normal lung fibroblasts (`GSE226249`, $n = 27$; $\Delta = -0.48$, paired $t = -10.89$, $P = 4.47 \times 10^{-6}$; core ECM $P = 1.73 \times 10^{-4}$), selective `TGFBR1`/ALK5 kinase inhibition by RepSox versus `TGF-beta1` stimulation (`GSE154646`, $n = 24$; core ECM $\Delta = -0.30$, paired $P = 1.10 \times 10^{-3}$; full 21-gene $P = 0.0101$), and nintedanib treatment (`GSE210020`, $n = 22$ wild-type samples of 40 total GSMs; core ECM $\Delta = -0.54$, Welch $P = 0.0182$). Across $n = 264$ metastatic melanoma patients managed without immune checkpoint blockade (`GSE65904` $n = 210$ of 214 GSMs [102 deaths] and `GSE22153` $n = 54$ of 57 GSMs [47 deaths]), locked stromal barrier load conferred zero excess mortality (`multivariable HR = 0.84`, 95% CI 0.56–1.25, $P = 0.389$), yielding a significant two-regime `anti-PD-1 x locked stromal barrier` interaction across all $n = 338$ melanoma patients (`192` deaths; `interaction HR = 3.60`, 95% CI 1.45–8.98, $Z = +2.75$, $P = 0.0059$). Across all 24 SHA-256-locked Phase IIb/III antifibrotic trials, macro `DNAcc` correlated monotonically with zero-label meso Itô–Langevin simulated remission (`Spearman` $\rho = 0.7861$, $P = 5.29 \times 10^{-6}$; `Pearson` $r = 0.6079$, $P = 0.0016$). Multi-gene target screens controlled false discovery rates (FDR) via the Benjamini–Hochberg procedure, single-cell and spatial reporting followed MINSEQE standards, and zero-label multicellular Langevin simulations adhered to an ASME V&V 40 verification, validation, and uncertainty quantification (VVUQ) Context of Use.
